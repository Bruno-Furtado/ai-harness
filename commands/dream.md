description: Run the dream memory routine or act on its pending proposals. Bare /dream runs a full pass; "apply 1,3" or "apply all" apply approved changes; "list" shows pending; "dismiss 1,3" discards.
---

Follow the `dream` skill.

`$ARGUMENTS` selects what to do:

- empty: run a full dream pass.
- `apply 1,3` or `apply all`: apply the numbered pending proposals.
- `list`: print the pending proposals.
- `dismiss 1,3`: discard the numbered pending proposals.

Deliver the scripts' stdout verbatim, the numbered list with its evidence included. Do not wrap it in a summary of your own.

If the `dream` skill is not available in this tool, say so and stop. Do not improvise the procedure, and never apply a memory change without an explicit apply.
